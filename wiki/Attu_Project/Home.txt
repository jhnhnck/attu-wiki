The '''Attu Project''' is a collaborative worldbuilding project where a group of individually controlled islands come together to create a shared world. The story and lore develop through this wiki and interactions between nation leaders on our Discord server.

If you are looking to learn more about the project, you can join us [https://links.attuproject.org/invite here]!

== Current Events ==
Current Year: {{#invoke:AttuCalendar|current_year}} PC

*Grand Magister [[Adisa Aram]] publicly resigns after she is exposed for election fraud.
*[[Cuscorrado Papregoli]] has been sworn in as [[Dux]] of [[La Rossa]].
*The government of the [[Collective Kelelemi Republic]] has collapsed following a vote of no confidence.
*The [[Qoeul-bo Hrnad]] has been scattered to the wind following the raid of [[Qoeul-bosken-ut]] and the capture and subsequent execution of [[Imam]] [[Sheg Praani]].
*[[42-Digging-Princepate/Historic-2627b5]] dropped his [[air frier]] courtside
