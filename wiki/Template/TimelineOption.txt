== TimelineOption ==

Configuration template for [[Module:Timeline]] data subpages. Sets rendering options for the timeline.

This template renders nothing when transcluded directly — it is read as raw wikitext by [[Module:Timeline]].

=== Usage ===

Place at the top of a timeline data subpage:

<pre>{{TimelineOption|key=value}}</pre>

=== Options ===

; calendar : date format used in {{[[Template:TimelineBar|TimelineBar]]}} date fields. Default: <code>haracalnde</code>.
:* <code>haracalnde</code> — <code>d-m y PC</code> / <code>d-m y TT</code> / <code>present</code>
:* <code>deysachni</code> — <code>year.month.day</code>, <code>year.month</code>, or <code>year</code> (e.g. <code>80.9.4</code>)

=== Example ===

<pre>{{TimelineOption|calendar=deysachni}}</pre>
