# Tietero

Working notes, assets, and tooling for the Kingdom of Tietero, an island nation within the Attu Project shared worldbuilding setting.

## The Attu Project

The [Attu Project](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Attu_Project:Guide) is a collaborative worldbuilding community. Each member designs and runs one island in a shared world; lore is developed on the wiki and through in-character interaction on Discord.

For most of recorded history, an unending storm raged across the archipelagos of an infinite ocean. Each island, isolated by the storm, developed its cultures, creatures, and ecosystems in complete independence. The storm subsided across the Attu Archipelago during the **Grand Calming**, opening contact between islands for the first time. A second calming opened the Brchipelago seas in 9 PC.

### World structure

- **Attu Archipelago:** Akaria, Casea, Eee, Faltir, Kelelemi, Niueyjar, Nongba, Tietero, Utlia.
- **Brchipelago:** Kalam, Okrit, La Rossa, Hapsaw, Deysachin, Isles of Joy, Steamworks, Spyron, Telaran, T'vaqi.
- **Crchipelago:** Walstanland, Skavn.
- **Destroyed:** Kiyut (sank).
- **Retconned / failed proposals:** Rockhome, Seatiean, Ishvara, Gonuela.

### Calendar

The **Haracalnde** calendar has 12 months of 30 days. The era before the Grand Calming is **Turbulence Time (TT)**, counted backward; the current era is **Post Calming (PC)**, counted forward. There is no year 0; the Grand Calming occurred in 1 PC, immediately following 1 TT. Dates are written `day-month year ERA`, e.g. `15-3 5 PC`. In-universe time is tracked by a Discord bot; each year typically lasts two weeks of real time.

### Technological ceiling

The project caps technology roughly at the 1970s. Notable exclusions: no fixed-wing aircraft using forward thrust for lift (no planes); no nuclear weapons larger than 20 kt.

### Moderation

A **Doom Committee** moderates the Discord, resolves disputes, and approves major lore changes. Inactive islands may be run by **Management Councils** which keep them as neutral NPC trading partners.

## Tietero

The **Kingdom of Tietero** ([Tieteran](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Tietero) pronunciation: [ˈtie.ˈtero]) is a parliamentary monarchy in the Attu Archipelago, off the southwestern coast of Akaria, its primary trading partner. The country comprises one main island and several barrier islands.

### Geography

A central mountainous range arcs from the southwest corner east, then north; rivers descend from it to the coast, seeding the major settlements. The climate is mostly temperate forest, with subtropical conditions and denser vegetation along the warmer west coast. [Mount Monstro](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Mount_Monstro) (3,185 m, in Lutroy canton) is the most prominent peak. Cities are built in brick and a volcanic-ash pseudo-concrete.

The country is divided into eight [cantons](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Cantons_of_Tietero), each semi-autonomous and structured to mirror the federal government: Kuniklos, Manatinow, Balenoj, [Kapitalo](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Zamenhof) (capital canton), Korvok, Lutroy, Strigot, Cervo.

### Government

Tietero is a parliamentary monarchy under the [Government Reorganization Act of 40 PC](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Government_Reorganization_Act_of_40_PC). Three branches:

- **Executive.** Headed by the [Reĝo](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Re%C4%9Do_of_Tietero) (hereditary, eldest legal child). Departments are led by Secretaries appointed by the monarch and confirmed by Parliament for five-year terms. A nationally elected **First Secretary** chairs the cabinet and the Senate; two-term limit.
- **Legislative.** Bicameral Parliament. The **Senate** has 32 seats; four are appointed by each cantonal government. The **House of Commons** uses Huntington-Hill apportionment (one seat per 100,000 people, post-53 PC rescaling; the pre-rescaling formula was one seat per ~6,250 people) with five-year party-list proportional elections and a five-term cap; it holds sole impeachment power.
- **Judicial.** Supreme Court (Chief Justice plus six Associate Justices, mandatory retirement at age 110), Cantonal Courts (ten-year renewable terms), and Local Courts (five-year renewable terms).

Executive departments include Defense, Education, Environment, Finance and Economy, Foreign Affairs, Health and Social Services, Infrastructure, and State. Defense subdivisions are the [Tieteran Royal Navy](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Tieteran_Royal_Navy), Royal Army, Coast Guard, [Tieteran Scouting Force](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Tieteran_Scouting_Force), and the [Bureau of Intelligence](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Intelligence). Mandatory military service runs from ages 14 to 18, largely coast-guard duty over the kelp fields.

Three registered parties as of the [42 PC general election](https://attuproject.org/wiki/42_PC_Tieteran_General_Election): the [Verdant](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Verdant) (environmentalist, anti-expansion), the [Integri](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Integri) (traditionalist, pro-monarchy), and the [Avanguardo](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Avanguardo) (technocratic, modernizing, pro-military).

### The Royal Family

The [House of Kunses](https://attuproject.org/wiki/House_of_Kunses) is the ruling dynasty. According to legend, a group of exiles abandoned at sea shipwrecked on Tietero; a child later born with extra fingers was read as a divine sign and crowned the first Reĝo, beginning a hereditary line marked by polydactyly.

Current line:

- [Alekso IV](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Alekso_IV), Reĝo de Tietero (b. 9-1 2 TT; reigning since 8-9 18 PC).
- Reĝido Mateo (Mateo Keating), Spozo de Tietero (married 10-2 35 PC).
- [Ellena](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Ellena), [Princino de Meduzoj](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Princino_de_Meduzoj) (b. 28-7 14 PC).
- Laverna, Princino de Balenoj (b. 3-11 40 PC; heir apparent).

Historical members:

### Economy

Chief exports are kelp and kelp-derived products (kelp oil, kelp-based textiles). Recreational drugs are legal and form a significant export and tourism sector; imports include manufactured consumer goods. Inland agriculture grows wheat, soy, and barley in stacked glass tube farms. See [islands/tietero/economy/exports.md](islands/tietero/economy/exports.md) and [Legalization of recreational drugs in Tietero](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Legalization_of_recreational_drugs_in_Tietero).

### Culture

Tieterans are born with six fingers per hand. One is amputated from the non-dominant hand at adulthood as a rite of passage; the elite have the procedure done surgically, while traditional practice uses communal bolt cutters. "Got the bolts" is a class insult. Historically the sixth finger of the dominant hand was amputated as criminal punishment; this was unintentionally banned by the Civil Rights Act of 27 PC and is now seen as archaic. Counting uses base-11.

Notable cultural pages: [Tieteran Pitball](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Tieteran_Pitball) (the national sport, played on long pitch-runways), [Gordo bread](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Gordo_bread), [Banoko cider](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Banoko_cider), [salted caramel](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Salted_caramel) (national candy), and the [timeslide](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Timeslide) (a traditional gyroscopic clock measuring sekendoj and heidoj).

### Religion

**[Portism](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Portism)** is the dominant tradition, treated more as cultural folklore than literal belief. It centers on the divine visit (around 2500 TT) of the gods **Filoto** and **Kapilano** and roughly fifty companions, who arrived on a sky vessel, built a runway, gave the Tieterans gifts of healing, food, gearworks, electricity, and meteorology, and taught equality and social welfare before ascending back to **Haveno**, the Sky's Sky. The visit is recorded in the *Aviado*, a book of oral testimonies compiled around 1450 TT by Aŭsteno I, the first Reĝo of the unified kingdom. Out of universe Portism is a cargo cult around aliens whose "sky vessels" were planes; in-universe, planes do not exist, so the irony is invisible to Tieterans.

Practice centers on maintaining grassy runways, hanging windsocks (a steady sock is good luck, a limp sock ill), and wearing the four-bladed propeller as a protective symbol. Burial cremates the head and launches the ashes in a skyfire firework at dusk to deliver the soul to Haveno; remaining ashes are used as binder in brickmaking. Clergy include canonized **Saints** in iridescent robes and translucent visors, and local **Regilo** who keep up the **Porto** (church) buildings and runways. **Landing Day** is the major holiday, falling on irregular intervals determined by moon, wind, and stars.

**[Discordism](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Discordism)** is a fringe but influential meta-religion holding that the world was created by malevolent higher beings within a "Discord" forum as a worldbuilding project. Tietero's Key Being, **Jhn**, is described as absent-minded and unserious. Adherents pursue inner peace and treat events as beyond their control.

### Folklore

[La Monstro](https://attuproject.org/wiki/La_Monstro) is a bipedal, blue-eyed, canine cryptid said to inhabit the mountainous interior. Sightings date to 670 TT and spike during periods of social or environmental distress. Cultural responses include protective wooden totems and food offerings; see also [Mount Monstro](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Mount_Monstro) and the [La Monstro sightings category](https://attuproject.org/wiki/Category:La_Monstro_sightings).
